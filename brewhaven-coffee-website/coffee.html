The roast spectrum

Roasting turns green coffee seeds into the brown beans we know. Longer roasting brings deeper, bolder flavors.

Blonde

Lightly roasted, mellow and bright.

  • Body: light
  • Acidity: higher
  • Notes: citrus, honey, toasted grain

Medium

Balanced, smooth and rich.

  • Body: medium
  • Acidity: balanced
  • Notes: chocolate, nuts, caramel

Dark

Bold, robust and full-bodied.

  • Body: full
  • Acidity: lower
  • Notes: dark cocoa, smoke, spice

Where coffee grows

Coffee grows in the "bean belt", the warm region around the equator. Each area gives beans a different character.

Latin America

Coffees from this region are often described as clean and balanced, with flavors of cocoa and toasted nuts.

  • Typical flavors: chocolate, nuts, soft fruit
  • Pairs with: cinnamon, caramel, milk chocolate
  • Try it as: a smooth medium roast

Africa

Coffee is believed to have first been discovered in East Africa. Coffees from the region are often vibrant and fruity.

  • Typical flavors: berries, citrus, florals
  • Pairs with: lemon, berries, dark chocolate
  • Try it as: a bright pour-over

Asia / Pacific

Island-grown coffees are often full-bodied and earthy, with herbal or spicy notes.

  • Typical flavors: earthy, herbal, spice
  • Pairs with: maple, cinnamon, savory food
  • Try it as: a bold French press

Brewing methods

Four popular ways to make coffee at home. A good starting ratio is about 1 gram of coffee to 16 grams of water.

Pour-over

Slowly pour hot water over medium-fine grounds in a filter. Clean and bright.

French press

Coffee basics

Great coffee comes down to four simple things. Get these right and almost any brewing method will taste better.

  • Proportion: start with about 2 tablespoons of ground coffee for every 180 ml (6 fl oz) of water, then adjust to taste.
  • Grind: match the grind to your method — coarse for French press and cold brew, medium for drip, fine for espresso.
  • Water: use fresh, filtered water just off the boil (around 90–96°C / 195–205°F).
  • Freshness: store beans in an airtight container away from light and heat, and grind just before brewing.
